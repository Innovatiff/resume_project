Logistics Coordinator
Company: Essex Fresh Distribution Inc.
Windsor, ON · Full-time · Posted 3 days ago
Salary $58,000 - $70,000 per year

About the role
Essex Fresh Distribution is a regional food distributor serving grocers across Southwestern Ontario. The Logistics Coordinator keeps product moving from receiving to the customer's dock.

Responsibilities
• Coordinate inbound and outbound shipments with carriers and drivers
• Maintain inventory accuracy in the WMS and run cycle counts
• Build daily delivery schedules and communicate changes to the floor
• Prepare KPI reporting for the operations manager

Requirements
• 3+ years in a warehouse, distribution or logistics role
• Hands-on experience with a WMS and cycle counting
• Strong Excel skills for scheduling and reporting
• Scheduling experience across multiple shifts
• Valid forklift licence
• Must be legally entitled to work in Canada

Nice to have
• SAP experience is an asset
• Bilingual English and Spanish is an asset
