Logistics Coordinator
Company: Great Lakes Fresh Foods LLC
Detroit, MI 48226 · Full-time · Posted 5 days ago
Salary $55,000 - $68,000 per year

About the role
Great Lakes Fresh Foods distributes produce to grocers across Michigan and Ohio. The Logistics Coordinator keeps product moving from receiving to the customer's dock.

Responsibilities
• Coordinate inbound and outbound shipments with carriers and drivers
• Maintain inventory accuracy in the WMS and run cycle counts
• Build daily delivery schedules and communicate changes to the floor
• Prepare KPI reporting for the operations manager

Requirements
• 3+ years in a warehouse, distribution or logistics role
• Hands-on experience with a WMS and cycle counting
• Strong Excel skills for scheduling and reporting
• Scheduling experience across multiple shifts
• Valid forklift certification
• Must be authorized to work in the United States

Nice to have
• SAP experience is a plus
• Bilingual English and Spanish is a plus
